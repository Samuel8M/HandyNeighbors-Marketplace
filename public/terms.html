Terms of Service

This is a draft, not reviewed by a lawyer. It's written to plainly describe how this portfolio project actually works. Before this platform is used with real transactions at any scale, it should be reviewed by an actual attorney — treat this page as a starting point, not a finished legal document.

What HandyNeighbors is

HandyNeighbors is a free directory where independent people ("workers") list handyman-type skills, equipment, and an hourly rate, and other people ("users") search, compare, and contact them directly. HandyNeighbors is not an employer, staffing agency, contractor, escrow service, or payment processor. It does not supervise, guarantee, insure, or bond any work performed.

Accounts

You need an account to post a listing or leave a review; browsing and searching don't require one. You're responsible for your password and for anything done through your account. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account.

What "Verified" means (and doesn't)

A "Verified" badge means only that HandyNeighbors confirmed the account holder controls the email address on file, by sending a link to it. It is not a background check, identity check, license check, insurance check, or any endorsement of the quality or safety of anyone's work. Confirm any licensing, insurance, or background information you care about directly with the person you're hiring.

Independent workers, not employees

Anyone listed on HandyNeighbors is an independent individual, not an employee, contractor, or agent of HandyNeighbors. HandyNeighbors takes no cut of any job and never handles payment — you and the person you hire arrange price, scope, and payment directly, entirely outside this platform.

Listings and reviews

Listings must describe your own real skills, equipment, and rate — no companies, no listings on behalf of someone else. Reviews must reflect a real interaction; you may not review your own listing, and each account may review a given listing once. HandyNeighbors may remove listings or reviews that violate these terms.

No warranty

HandyNeighbors is provided "as is," with no warranty of any kind. HandyNeighbors is not liable for the quality, safety, legality, or outcome of any work arranged through the platform, or for any dispute between a user and a worker.

Changes

These terms may change as the project changes. Continuing to use HandyNeighbors after a change means you accept the new terms.
